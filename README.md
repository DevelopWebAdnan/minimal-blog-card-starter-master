### What I learned

<!-- Use this section to recap over some of your major learnings while working through this project. Writing these out and providing code samples of areas you want to highlight is a great way to reinforce your own knowledge. -->
I learned that we may sometimes think that we may not need to use one of the provided colors in our site, but if we look at the provided design thoroughly, we will find out that the color is used somewhere in it. As for example, I thought that the section containing the card had no background color, and so one of the provided colors seemed to be unnecessary. Actually the greatest challenge was to find out that there was a background color for the overall section containing the card and it was that color which I thought was unnecessarily provided. I also found out that the line-height property may be helpful at one or two situations when the default line-height of a paragraph or list items (as in the previous challenge) is not equal to, or does not match with the provided design.
